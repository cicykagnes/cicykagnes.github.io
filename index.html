Home

I'm an M.Sc. Computer Science student at Saarland University and a Research Assistant in the Language Technology Lab at the German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI), where I evaluate multilingual language models for robustness and cultural bias in the TRAILS project. Before that, I spent three years in DFKI's Smart Service Engineering Lab working on knowledge graphs, factuality and energy-efficient AI systems.

My research is about evaluation that can be trusted for safety: anticipating how language models fail, especially when the evaluator is itself a model and when the evidence of failure lies inside the model rather than in its output. One observation drives most of my work: the signals we use to decide whether a model is right, its confidence or its agreement with other models, can be high exactly when it is wrong.

Research interests:

  • Evaluating the evaluators: reliability and shared biases of LLM judges (length, position, self-preference), and bias-aware ranking from pairwise verdicts
  • Failure from the inside: detecting confident hallucinations from internal representations, and scoring the reliability of individual judge verdicts
  • Systems that act: safety-constrained agents, and the LLM monitors that oversee them
  • Robustness across languages: cultural bias and robustness of multilingual models
