Under review

Jury of Unequals: Improving Ranking in LLM-as-a-Judge Frameworks
C. K. Agnes, D. A. Selby, S. Vollmer, G. Großmann

Ties each model's reliability as a judge to its ability as an answerer with one shared latent variable. No extra parameters over Bradley–Terry–Luce, and up to 56% lower rank error.

Contrastive Representational Decoding for Hallucination Detection
C. K. Agnes
NeurIPS 2026Women in Machine Learning Workshop

Decodes hallucinations from hidden states across transformer layers with a contrastive projection: AUROC 0.945 on TruthfulQA with about 32 labelled examples.
